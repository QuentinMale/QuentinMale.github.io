# Student projects

I welcome applications for several MSc thesis projects within the Department of Engineering at the University of Luxembourg, starting early 2027. Proposed topics will focus on developing machine learning methods to improve the simulation of multiphysics flows (turbulence, combustion, plasma, etc.). These projects are inherently interdisciplinary, spanning machine learning, physics, and high-performance computing. Feel free to reach out by [email](mailto:qmale@seas.harvard.edu) if you are interested.
