# Open positions

## PhD position — Department of Engineering, University of Luxembourg

I am recruiting a doctoral candidate to join my group in the Department of Engineering at the University of Luxembourg, with an expected start in 2027. The project will focus on an interdisciplinary topic combining machine learning, high-performance computing and fluid dynamics.

Interested applicants are welcome to reach out by [email](mailto:contact@quentinmale.com) to discuss the position.

## PhD position — YADIAS Doctoral Fellow Programme

I am looking for candidates interested in pursuing a PhD at the interface of physics, machine learning, and high-performance computing.

The position is expected to be funded through the [YADIAS Doctoral Fellow Programme](https://yadias.uni.lu), which promotes interdisciplinary research and international collaboration. The project would be hosted at the University of Luxembourg, with international collaboration involving Harvard University ([CSElab](https://cse-lab.seas.harvard.edu)). The exact research project will be defined together with the selected candidate. Potential application areas include turbulent flows, combustion, plasma physics, inverse problems, scientific computing, and multiphysics modelling. Applicants should hold an MSc degree by the application deadline (November 2026).

If you are interested please feel free to get in touch by [email](mailto:contact@quentinmale.com). I would be happy to discuss potential research directions and answer any questions.

## Postdoctoral researcher positions — external funding opportunities

I welcome researchers seeking external funding to join my group at the University of Luxembourg. I will help develop proposals on shared interests, provide mentoring throughout the application and fellowship, and integrate successful candidates into the lab's research programme and collaborative environment.

Potential funding routes include the [FNR CORE Junior track](https://www.fnr.lu/funding-instruments/core) and [Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships). Other schemes may be considered depending on the candidate's profile and eligibility.

Interested candidates should [email me](mailto:contact@quentinmale.com) well before the deadline with a CV, a brief description of their research interests, how these align with my group's work, and the intended funding scheme.
